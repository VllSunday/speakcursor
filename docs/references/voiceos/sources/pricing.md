# VoiceOS pricing

VoiceOS offers paid Pro and custom Enterprise plans for Mac and Windows. There is no free sign-up plan.

Updated: October 2, 2026.

## Free trial

New customers receive a 7-day free trial of Pro on either the monthly or annual plan. A card is required. Cancel before the trial ends to avoid a charge. One trial per email address; previous subscribers are not eligible for another trial. After seven days, your chosen plan starts automatically at the price shown at checkout.

## Pro

US reference prices: $29.99 billed monthly, or $143.88 billed annually (equivalent to $11.99 per month). Confirm regional prices and billing terms at checkout.

Includes unlimited Dictation and Agent Mode usage, selected-text editing, custom vocabulary, and priority support. Features require the applicable permissions and supported app connections.

## Enterprise

Custom pricing. Contact VoiceOS to confirm seats, administration, retention terms, and identity-provider requirements, including SSO/SAML. Available contractual terms must be confirmed for the deployment.

## Existing accounts after cancellation

When paid Pro access ends after cancellation, existing accounts have limited access: 100 dictation sessions and 25 Agent Mode sessions per week. This entitlement is not a plan a new customer can select.

## Privacy

Cloud processing and retention are separate. Help Improve VoiceOS changes collection and training permissions. See the [Privacy Policy](https://www.voiceos.com/privacy) for the full rules.
