Questions?
How is VoiceOS different from traditional speech recognition?
VoiceOS uses advanced AI to go beyond basic speech-to-text. While Mac Dictation transcribes exactly what you say (including 'um' and 'uh'), VoiceOS automatically removes filler words, fixes grammar, and formats your text professionally. It's like having an editor built in, perfect for emails, documents, and messaging.
Is my data private and secure with VoiceOS?
Absolutely. VoiceOS processes your audio in real time and never stores it unless you give permission to help improve the product. Your transcripts are saved locally on your device, giving you full control over them. None of your data is used for training or shared with anyone. Privacy and security are built into everything we do.
What apps does VoiceOS work with?
VoiceOS works seamlessly across your favorite apps like Gmail, Slack, Notion, Google Docs, iMessage, WhatsApp, and more. Whether you're writing code in Cursor, chatting in Messenger, or drafting in Obsidian, VoiceOS is there to help you communicate faster.
