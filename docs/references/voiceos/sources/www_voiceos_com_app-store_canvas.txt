Getting started
1Download VoiceOSFree to start, on Mac and Windows.
2Connect CanvasOne click in the dashboard's Integrations tab.
3Just say itSpeak — VoiceOS does the rest.
Connects to your Canvas account — you sign in with Canvas in your browser, and VoiceOS never sees your password.
Canvas needs an access token from your school account — VoiceOS walks you through creating one when you connect.
VoiceOS asks you to confirm before it sends, changes, or deletes anything — every action marked “Asks first” below.
