Getting started
1Download VoiceOSFree to start, on Mac and Windows.
2Connect JiraOne click in the dashboard's Integrations tab.
3Just say itSpeak — VoiceOS does the rest.
Connects to your Jira account — you sign in with Jira in your browser, and VoiceOS never sees your password.
Your connection is bound to one Atlassian workspace, shown on the integration card so you always know where actions run.
VoiceOS asks you to confirm before it sends, changes, or deletes anything — every action marked “Asks first” below.
