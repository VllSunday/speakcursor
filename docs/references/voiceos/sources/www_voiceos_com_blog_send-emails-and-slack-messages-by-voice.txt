Send messages by voice without opening the app
Here is the part most voice tools cannot do: you do not have to open Gmail, Outlook, Slack, or Microsoft Teams to send a message. VoiceOS is an AI voice assistant for your computer that turns voice to action, so the app stays closed. You stay in whatever you were doing, whether that is writing in a doc, reviewing code, or reading an article, and you simply speak the message you want sent.
Say something like "reply to my boss that I can't make the meeting tomorrow." VoiceOS understands who you mean, writes a complete, properly worded draft, and shows it to you in a small confirmation card right where you are. You read it, then send it. That is the entire workflow, and it never pulls you out of your current window.
This is what makes Agent Mode different from dictation. Dictation needs you to already be in the message field. Agent Mode brings the message to you and handles the sending. For more on this distinction, see voice AI agents versus dictation.
How to send an email by voice
You can send an email by voice from anywhere on your computer, without opening your email app. It works whether you use Gmail or Outlook. Here is the Agent Mode flow.
1
Trigger VoiceOS from any app
Press your VoiceOS trigger key while you are in any window. You do not need to switch to Gmail, Outlook, or Apple Mail first.
2
Speak your command
Say what you want to happen in plain language: "Reply to my boss that I can't make the meeting tomorrow, and suggest we move it to Thursday." Or start a new one: "Email hello@voiceos.com that the proposal is ready for review."
3
VoiceOS writes the draft
Agent Mode composes a complete, professionally worded email and shows you a confirmation card with the recipient, subject, and a preview of the message.
4
Confirm by click or voice
If it looks good, click Send or just say so. Need a change? Say "edit" and tell VoiceOS what to fix. Nothing leaves your computer until you approve it.
How to send a Slack or Teams message by voice
Team chat works exactly the same way. Whether you use Slack or Microsoft Teams, you never open the app or click into a channel. Agent Mode handles finding the right place and posting the message.
VoiceOS drafts your Slack message and shows a confirmation card so you can review the channel and text before it sends. The same flow works for Microsoft Teams.
1
Trigger VoiceOS from wherever you are
No need to switch to Slack or Teams. Activate VoiceOS from your current app.
2
Say your message
For example: "Reply to my boss on Slack that I can't make the meeting," or "Send a Teams message to the engineering channel that the deploy is done."
3
VoiceOS finds the channel and drafts it
Agent Mode identifies the right person or channel in Slack or Microsoft Teams, writes the message in a casual chat tone, and shows you the preview.
4
Confirm and it posts
Approve by click or by voice and the message is sent. Same hands-free flow as email.
You always confirm before anything sends
Agent Mode never sends a message on its own. Every draft appears in a confirmation card first, so you stay fully in control. You have three options at that point:
Send
Click the Send button, or confirm out loud. The message goes out immediately.
Edit
Say "edit" and tell VoiceOS what to change, like "make it more formal" or "add that I will send notes afterward." It rewrites the draft and shows it again.
Cancel
Dismiss the card and nothing is sent. No message is ever delivered without your explicit approval.
Dictation vs Agent Mode
Most voice tools only do dictation. They turn your speech into text, but you still have to open the app, click into the field, and press send yourself. That is helpful when you are already composing a message, and VoiceOS does dictation too: open any text field, speak, and it types polished text with filler words removed and grammar fixed.
Agent Mode is the upgrade. Instead of just producing text, it performs the task. It opens the right conversation behind the scenes, drafts the message, and sends it after you confirm. You can even chain steps in one command: "check the weather for Saturday, email the team suggesting a beach day with the forecast, and create a calendar event for it." One spoken request, multiple actions, all confirmed before anything runs.

Tips for accurate voice messages
Name the recipient and the app in your command, like "reply to my boss on Slack," so Agent Mode routes it correctly.
Speak the intent, not the exact wording. Say "tell the team I'll be 10 minutes late" and let VoiceOS phrase it.
Add names and jargon to your custom dictionary so colleague names, product names, and acronyms are spelled right every time.
Use the preview. Glance at the confirmation card before sending, especially for important recipients.
Confirm by voice when your hands are busy, so the whole flow stays hands-free.
