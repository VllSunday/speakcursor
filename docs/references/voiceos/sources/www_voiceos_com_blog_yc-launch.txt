The problem: Typing sucks
Most people use their keyboard hundreds of times a day, and the costs add up. Every email, every Slack message, every document means translating the thought in your head into keystrokes on a screen. It's a process so ingrained we barely notice how much it slows us down.
Mental overhead — turning thoughts into typed words
Time wasted — correcting your typos
Lost momentum — when context-switching between apps
We've all experienced it: you know exactly what you want to say, but by the time you type it out, rephrase it, fix the typos, and polish the formatting, the original spark has faded. The keyboard, for all its utility, is a bottleneck between your brain and the digital world.
The solution: Voice as the new OS
VoiceOS is a universal voice interface that converts thoughts into polished output, instantly. Instead of typing out every word, you simply speak your intent and VoiceOS handles the rest: formatting, tone, grammar, and context.
Reply instantly
"Ask to reschedule for tomorrow" → replied instantly. No typing, no overthinking.
Better prompting
Turn messy thoughts into perfect prompts for AI tools, effortlessly.
Works everywhere
Works with any app: Slack, Gmail, Notion, ChatGPT, Cursor. Zero setup required.
We're building for a world where AGI is no longer science fiction but an emerging roadmap. In that world, the keyboard won't be the primary way you interact with technology. Your voice will be.
The team: Voice AI Pioneers
Over the past 7 years, Kai and Jonah have built voice AI solutions, from consumer products to Fortune 500 deployments. That experience taught them one thing: voice technology has been held back not by the models, but by the interfaces. The world needed a voice operating system, one designed from the ground up for productivity, not just dictation.
That conviction led them to build VoiceOS and bring it to Y Combinator. Today, they're sharing it with the world.
Why now
Voice technology has reached an inflection point. Speech recognition accuracy has crossed 95%, large language models can understand context and intent, and real-time processing is fast enough for conversation-speed interactions. For the first time, it's possible to build a voice interface that doesn't just transcribe: it understands.
VoiceOS is built on this convergence. We combine state-of-the-art speech recognition with AI post-processing to deliver output that reads like you typed it carefully, even though you just spoke naturally. The result is a tool that makes voice input feel effortless, not experimental.
Our ask
We launched on Y Combinator and the response has been incredible. You can check out our YC launch page to see the demo video and learn more about what we're building.
If you haven't tried VoiceOS yet, we'd love for you to give it a shot. Download the app and use it in every app you already have. No plugins, no extensions, no configuration. Just speak.
