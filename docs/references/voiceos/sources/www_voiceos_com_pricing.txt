Frequently asked questions
Is there a free trial?
Yes. Every new account starts with a 7-day free trial of Pro. A card is required at checkout, and you can cancel any time before the trial ends. One trial per email address.
What happens when the trial ends?
Your plan starts automatically on the billing cycle you chose at checkout, monthly or annual, at the price shown there. When the trial begins we email you the date and the amount of that first charge, so nothing arrives as a surprise.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes. Cancel from Settings, then Billing inside the app. You keep Pro until the end of the period you have already paid for.
What happens to my account if I cancel?
After cancellation, when your paid Pro period ends, existing accounts have limited access: 100 dictation sessions and 25 Agent Mode sessions per week. This is not a plan available at sign-up.
Do you offer discounts?
Students get 50% off Pro while enrolled, and we re-check enrollment once a year. Builders shipping their own VoiceOS integrations can apply for 50% off Pro. Both are short application forms that we read by hand.
What is the difference between monthly and annual?
Annual is billed once a year and works out to 60% less per month than paying monthly. The prices on the cards above update to your region automatically.
Which platforms does VoiceOS run on?
Mac and Windows today. Mobile is on the way and you can join the waitlist from the mobile page.
Do you have plans for teams?
VoiceOS offers Enterprise for teams. Contact us to confirm available administration, retention, and identity-provider requirements in your agreement.
