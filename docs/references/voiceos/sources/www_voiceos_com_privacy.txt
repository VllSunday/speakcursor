1. Who We Are and What This Policy Covers
VoiceOS is operated by WakoAI Inc., located at 35 Buckelew Street, Sausalito, California 94965, United States.
This Privacy Policy explains how WakoAI Inc. collects, uses, retains, protects, and shares personal information when you use the VoiceOS website, macOS application, Windows application, dashboard, Team plans, Enterprise plans, and affiliate or referral program. Together, these are the Service.
This Privacy Policy does not cover a separate VoiceOS mobile application. If WakoAI Inc. launches a mobile application, it will provide the notice applicable to that product before launch.
If you have questions about this policy or want to exercise a privacy choice, contact us at support@voiceos.com.
2. Plain Language Summary
VoiceOS lets you dictate text and use Agent Mode to carry out user-directed tasks. To provide those features, VoiceOS processes the audio, text, and limited context you choose to provide.
By default, VoiceOS does not use your raw audio, transcripts, edited text, Agent prompts, Agent responses, data supplied to Agent Mode, or Agent outputs to evaluate, train, or improve VoiceOS models. Raw audio and transcripts are deleted immediately after processing needed to provide the requested feature and are not saved when Help Improve VoiceOS is off.
If you affirmatively enable Help Improve VoiceOS, VoiceOS may use the content categories described in Section 4 for evaluation. Raw audio and transcripts collected under that setting may be retained indefinitely for training. Turning the setting off later stops future collection under that setting but does not delete information already collected under it.
VoiceOS retains limited operational information needed to run, secure, support, measure, and administer the Service. This can include account identifiers, subscription status, usage counters, consent records, security logs, crash reports, diagnostics, user-submitted support communications, and limited referral information.

4. Help Improve VoiceOS
4.1 When Help Improve VoiceOS Is Off
When Help Improve VoiceOS is off, VoiceOS does not use your raw audio, transcripts, edited text, Agent prompts, Agent responses, data you provide to Agent Mode, or Agent outputs to evaluate, train, fine tune, benchmark, or otherwise improve VoiceOS models.
VoiceOS still processes information necessary to provide a feature you request. For example, it may transmit audio to a speech-processing provider or transmit a prompt and context to an AI-processing provider so that Dictation Mode or Agent Mode can work. This service-delivery processing is separate from using content for VoiceOS evaluation or training.
When Help Improve VoiceOS is off, raw audio and transcripts are deleted immediately after processing and are not saved by VoiceOS.
4.2 When Help Improve VoiceOS Is On
Help Improve VoiceOS is an optional setting. If you turn it on, VoiceOS may use raw audio, transcripts, edited text, Agent prompts, Agent responses, data you provide to Agent Mode, and Agent outputs for evaluation of VoiceOS.
Raw audio and transcripts collected while Help Improve VoiceOS is on may be retained indefinitely for training. Before you enable the setting, VoiceOS will present the setting and its effect in the product.
4.3 Turning the Setting Off
If you turn Help Improve VoiceOS off after previously enabling it, VoiceOS stops future collection under that setting. Information collected while the setting was on remains retained under the applicable Help Improve VoiceOS retention rule. Turning the setting off does not retrospectively delete content already collected for training or evaluation.
5. Agent Mode, Context, and Integrations
5.1 Agent Mode Context
Agent Mode may process selected text, screenshots you choose to allow, drawn screen regions, files you attach, connected-service content, web-search results, and tool inputs and outputs when needed to complete a task you request.
After processing, VoiceOS does not retain the details of those requests, screens, tool calls, MCP interactions, outputs, or other Agent Mode content. VoiceOS may retain limited non-content operational metadata, such as the number of requests, number of outputs, error indicators, and the request type, such as a search, screen-view, or specific MCP request.
5.2 Connected Services
You may connect third-party services, such as email, chat, calendar, files, notes, and issue-tracking services. When you connect a service, VoiceOS uses the permissions and credentials you grant to carry out the actions you request.
You can disconnect a connected service through VoiceOS and may also revoke VoiceOS access through the third-party service. Third-party services you connect are governed by their own terms and privacy notices.
5.3 Custom MCP Servers
You may add your own Model Context Protocol, or MCP, servers as local commands or remote URLs. Custom MCP servers are third-party tools and services that you choose to enable. VoiceOS does not control, operate, or secure them. When Agent Mode uses a custom MCP server, your instruction and any context needed for that tool call may be transmitted to the endpoint you configured.
6. How We Share Information
VoiceOS shares personal information only as needed to provide the Service, conduct a transaction you request, operate the business, or comply with law. We may share information with the following categories of recipients:
cloud infrastructure, database, and authentication providers;
speech-recognition, AI-processing, and text-to-speech providers;
payment and subscription processors;
integration and OAuth providers that help connect services you choose;
analytics, error-monitoring, referral, attribution, and advertising-measurement providers;
email, support, community, search, maps, and media providers when you use the relevant feature;
professional advisors, insurers, auditors, and service providers under appropriate confidentiality obligations;
authorities or other parties when required by law or necessary to protect rights, safety, security, or the Service; and
a successor or acquirer in connection with a merger, financing, acquisition, restructuring, or sale of assets, subject to appropriate protections.
We use service providers that may include AWS, Supabase, Stripe, AI and voice-processing providers, Composio, PostHog, Sentry, Resend, Dub, Google Tag Manager, OpenAI Ads measurement tools, Exa, maps providers, Spotify, Discord, and software-signing or distribution providers. A provider receives information only when it is needed for the relevant feature, transaction, or operational function.
7. Website Analytics, Advertising, and Referrals
VoiceOS uses PostHog, Sentry, Dub, Google Tag Manager, OpenAI Ads measurement tools, UTM parameters, and similar technologies to operate the website, understand product use, measure campaigns, attribute referrals, and diagnose errors.
VoiceOS does not use PostHog session replay and does not record screen sessions through PostHog. UTM parameters are used for campaign attribution and do not include your voice, transcript, Agent Mode content, or screen content.
VoiceOS responds to browser Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control signals. You can manage cookies through your browser settings.
8. Team and Enterprise Plans
If you use VoiceOS through a Team or Enterprise plan, organization administrators may manage seats and billing. Administrators can access shared dictionaries, replacements, shared Memories, and integrations made available to the organization. Administrators do not receive access to your individual activity or aggregate usage through the ordinary Team or Enterprise administrative surfaces.
Enterprise customers may request a separate Enterprise Agreement or Data Processing Agreement. If WakoAI Inc. and an Enterprise customer sign one, its privacy and data-handling terms may supplement this policy and control to the extent of a conflict.
9. Retention and Account Deletion
VoiceOS retains personal information for as long as necessary to provide the Service and fulfill the purposes described in this policy, unless a longer period is required or permitted by law.
When you delete your account, VoiceOS deletes account-linked stored data, including applicable Memories, knowledge entries, dictionary entries, replacements, writing styles, Team-shared data associated with your account, and account-linked logs, within 40 days. This does not require VoiceOS to delete deidentified or aggregated numerical statistics, such as total request counts or total subscription counts, that no longer identify you.
The Help Improve VoiceOS rules in Section 4 apply separately to raw audio and transcripts collected while that setting is on. In particular, turning off the setting does not delete information previously collected under the setting.
10. Data Deletion Procedure
You may request deletion of your personal information at any time by:
Deleting your account through Settings in VoiceOS, which triggers automatic deletion of account-linked data as described in Section 9; or
Submitting a deletion request by emailing support@voiceos.com with the subject line "Data Deletion Request" and enough information for us to identify your account.
Upon receiving a verified deletion request, WakoAI Inc. will:
Delete your account-linked stored data, including Memories, knowledge entries, dictionary entries, replacements, writing styles, transcripts, and session data, within 40 days.
Delete associated audio recordings stored in our file storage systems within 40 days.
Cancel any active subscription and remove payment method references from our systems (payment history retained by our payment processor as required by financial regulations).
Remove your data from active analytics systems. Deidentified or aggregated statistics that no longer identify you may be retained.
Retain a minimal record (email address and deletion timestamp) solely for fraud prevention and trial-abuse detection, as disclosed in Section 9.
We will confirm completion of the deletion by email. If we cannot fulfill a request due to a legal obligation or legitimate exception, we will inform you of the reason and the specific data retained.
The Help Improve VoiceOS rules in Section 4 apply separately. Content collected while that setting was on is not deleted by account deletion or a deletion request, as described in Section 4.3.
11. Security
VoiceOS uses reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect personal information. No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.
12. Your Choices and Privacy Requests
You may:
turn Help Improve VoiceOS on or off;
delete account-linked data by closing your account;
manage or disconnect connected services;
remove custom MCP servers;
manage Memories, knowledge entries, dictionaries, replacements, writing styles, and shared resources available to you;
use browser controls to manage cookies and similar technologies; and
contact support to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information, subject to applicable law and verification.
To make a privacy request, contact support@voiceos.com and include enough information for us to identify your account and process the request.
13. Children and Eligibility
VoiceOS is not directed to children under 13. You must be at least 13 years old to use the Service, or older if the law where you live requires a higher minimum age, such as 16 in certain regions. If you believe a child has provided personal information to VoiceOS in violation of this policy, contact us at support@voiceos.com.
14. Changes to This Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make a material change, we will update the Last updated date and may provide additional notice through the Service or by email when appropriate.
